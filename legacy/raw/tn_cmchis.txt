[SECTION 1] Overview
The Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS), co-branded as AB-PMJAY-CMCHIS since its 2018 integration with the central Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme, provides cashless health insurance coverage to eligible Tamil Nadu families for a defined list of medical and surgical procedures at network hospitals, as notified under GO(Ms) No.560, Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Tamil Nadu.

[SECTION 2] Benefit amount
Eligible families receive cashless treatment coverage of up to Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year across the scheme's listed procedures.

[SECTION 3.1] Eligibility - Residency
The applicant must be a resident of Tamil Nadu.

[SECTION 3.2] Eligibility - Family ration card
The applicant's name must appear on the family's ration card issued in Tamil Nadu.

[SECTION 3.3] Eligibility - Income threshold
The applicant's annual family income must be under Rs. 1,20,000, as notified under GO(Ms) No.560, Health and Family Welfare Department.
